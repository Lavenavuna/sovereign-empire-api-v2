\## 🎯 Overview



This document provides a clear, step-by-step action plan to ensure Sovereign Empire AI remains fully compliant with FRCS requirements at every stage of business growth.



\---



\## 📊 The 4-Stage Compliance Roadmap



\### Stage 1: Startup (Revenue: $0 - $50,000)



\*\*Focus:\*\* Business registration and basic compliance



| Action | Deadline | Status |

|--------|----------|--------|

| Register business with ROC | Week 1 | ⏳ |

| Obtain Certificate of Registration | Week 1 | ⏳ |

| Register for TIN on TPOS | Week 1 | ⏳ |

| Submit TIN letter to ROC | Week 1 | ⏳ |

| Open business bank account | Week 2 | ⏳ |

| Set up accounting system | Week 2 | ⏳ |



\*\*Key Responsibility:\*\* Business Owner



\*\*Monitoring:\*\* Monthly turnover tracking



\---



\### Stage 2: Growth (Revenue: $50,000 - $100,000)



\*\*Focus:\*\* VAT Monitoring System (VMS) registration



| Action | Deadline | Status |

|--------|----------|--------|

| Register for VMS | When turnover reaches $50,000 | ⏳ |

| Monitor turnover monthly | Ongoing | ⏳ |

| Prepare for VAT registration | Ongoing | ⏳ |



\*\*Key Responsibility:\*\* Business Owner / Accountant



\*\*Monitoring:\*\* Monthly turnover tracking



\---



\### Stage 3: VAT Registration (Revenue: $100,000+)



\*\*Focus:\*\* VAT registration and compliance



| Action | Deadline | Status |

|--------|----------|--------|

| Register for VAT | Within 21 days of reaching $100,000 | ⏳ |

| Start charging VAT on invoices | Immediately after registration | ⏳ |

| File first VAT return | Following month (28th) | ⏳ |

| Claim input tax credits | On each VAT return | ⏳ |



\*\*Key Responsibility:\*\* Business Owner / Accountant



\*\*Monitoring:\*\* Monthly VAT return filing



\---



\### Stage 4: Employment (When Hiring Employees)



\*\*Focus:\*\* PAYE, FNPF, and employer compliance



| Action | Deadline | Status |

|--------|----------|--------|

| Register as employer for PAYE | Within 30 days of first employee | ⏳ |

| Register with FNPF | Within 30 days of first employee | ⏳ |

| Register for FBT (if applicable) | Within 30 days | ⏳ |

| Submit first PAYE return | 14th of following month | ⏳ |

| Submit first FNPF contribution | Last day of month | ⏳ |



\*\*Key Responsibility:\*\* HR / Employer



\*\*Monitoring:\*\* Monthly payroll compliance

\## ✅ Checklist: When You Reach $100,000



\### Immediate Actions (Within 21 Days)



\- \[ ] Register for VAT on TPOS

\- \[ ] Update invoices to include VAT

\- \[ ] Set up VAT account

\- \[ ] Notify customers of VAT registration

\- \[ ] Review pricing (include VAT)



\### First VAT Return Actions



\- \[ ] Collect all tax invoices

\- \[ ] Calculate VAT collected (output tax)

\- \[ ] Calculate VAT paid (input tax)

\- \[ ] Complete VAT return form

\- \[ ] Submit by 28th of following month



\### Ongoing VAT Compliance



\- \[ ] Issue tax invoices for all sales

\- \[ ] Keep all tax invoices received

\- \[ ] File returns on time

\- \[ ] Pay VAT due on time



\---



\## ⚠️ Warning Signs: When to Seek Help



| Situation | Action |

|-----------|--------|

| \*\*Turnover approaching $100,000\*\* | Contact accountant for VAT preparation |

| \*\*Hiring first employee\*\* | Contact accountant for PAYE setup |

| \*\*Unclear about tax obligations\*\* | Contact FRCS or accountant |

| \*\*Missed filing deadline\*\* | Contact FRCS immediately |

| \*\*Received penalty notice\*\* | Contact FRCS and accountant |

\## 💡 Tips for Success



1\. \*\*Start with good habits\*\*: Keep records from day one

2\. \*\*Use accounting software\*\*: QuickBooks, Xero, or MYOB

3\. \*\*Set calendar reminders\*\*: For all filing deadlines

4\. \*\*Keep digital backups\*\*: Scan all documents

5\. \*\*Seek professional advice\*\*: When unsure about tax obligations
