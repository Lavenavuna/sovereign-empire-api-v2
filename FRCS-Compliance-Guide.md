\## 🏢 Business Registration



\### Step 1: Register with Registrar of Companies (ROC)



\*\*Action Required:\*\*

\- Register your business name and company structure

\- Obtain Certificate of Registration



\*\*Timeline:\*\* 1-3 working days



\*\*Cost:\*\* Registration fees apply



\### Step 2: Certificate of Registration



\*\*What You Need:\*\*

\- Company name approval

\- Memorandum \& Articles of Association

\- Director and shareholder details

\- Registered office address



\*\*Keep This Safe:\*\* This is required for all FRCS registrations



\---



\## 🆔 TIN Registration



\### When to Register



\*\*Immediately\*\* after ROC registration. You cannot operate without a TIN.



\### How to Register



1\. Go to \[TPOS Portal](https://tpos.frcs.org.fj)

2\. Register as a new taxpayer

3\. Complete all required fields

4\. Upload Certificate of Registration

5\. Submit application



\### Documents Needed



\- Certificate of Registration from ROC

\- Business profile

\- Director identification (Voter ID/Passport)



\### Timeline



\- \*\*Registration:\*\* 1-2 working days

\- \*\*TIN Issued:\*\* Immediately upon approval



\### ⚠️ Critical Note



\*\*You must log in to TPOS within 7 days of receiving your credentials, or your account will expire.\*\*

\## 💰 VAT Registration \& Compliance



\### VAT Registration Threshold



| Scenario | Turnover | Action Required |

|----------|----------|-----------------|

| \*\*Below Threshold\*\* | Less than $100,000/year | No registration required (optional) |

| \*\*Reached Threshold\*\* | $100,000/year or more | Register within 21 days |

| \*\*Expected Threshold\*\* | Expect to exceed $100,000 in next 12 months | Register immediately |



\### When to Register



\*\*Register within 21 days\*\* of:

1\. Reaching $100,000 annual turnover

2\. Expecting to exceed $100,000 in the next 12 months

3\. Making taxable supplies



\### VAT Filing Frequency



| Annual Turnover | Filing Frequency | Deadline |

|-----------------|------------------|----------|

| \*\*Over $300,000\*\* | Monthly (Category A) | 28th of following month |

| \*\*Under $300,000\*\* | Quarterly (Category B) | 28th of following month |



\### VAT Rate



| Rate | Applies To |

|------|------------|

| \*\*15%\*\* | All taxable supplies |

| \*\*0%\*\* | Exports and certain exempt supplies |



\### What You Can Claim



| Expense Type | Can Claim VAT? |

|--------------|----------------|

| \*\*Business purchases\*\* | ✅ Yes (with valid tax invoice) |

| \*\*Capital purchases\*\* | ✅ Yes (with valid tax invoice) |

| \*\*Personal expenses\*\* | ❌ No |

| \*\*Non-registered supplier purchases\*\* | ❌ No |



\### VAT Monitoring System (VMS)



\- The VMS threshold is set at \*\*$50,000\*\*

\- This is separate from VAT registration threshold

\- Must register if your turnover exceeds $50,000 even if not VAT registered

\## 👨‍💼 Employer Obligations (PAYE \& FNPF)



\### When to Register as an Employer



\*\*Within 30 days\*\* of hiring your first employee.



\### PAYE (Pay As You Earn)



| Requirement | Details |

|-------------|---------|

| \*\*Registration\*\* | Register on TPOS within 30 days of first employee |

| \*\*Filing Frequency\*\* | Monthly |

| \*\*Filing Deadline\*\* | 14th of following month |

| \*\*Record Keeping\*\* | 7 years |



\### PAYE Tax Rates (2026)



| Annual Income (FJD) | Tax Rate |

|---------------------|----------|

| Up to $30,000 | 0% |

| $30,001 - $50,000 | 18% |

| $50,001 - $270,000 | 20% |

| Over $270,000 | 20% |



\### FNPF (Fiji National Provident Fund)



| Requirement | Details |

|-------------|---------|

| \*\*Employer Registration\*\* | Within 30 days of first employee |

| \*\*Employee Registration\*\* | Within 1 month of employment |

| \*\*Employer Contribution\*\* | 10% of salary |

| \*\*Employee Contribution\*\* | 8% of salary |

| \*\*Contribution Deadline\*\* | Last day of each month |

| \*\*Record Keeping\*\* | 7 years |



\### FNPF Contribution Summary



| Contributor | Rate | On What |

|-------------|------|---------|

| \*\*Employer\*\* | 10% | Basic salary |

| \*\*Employee\*\* | 8% | Basic salary |

| \*\*Total\*\* | 18% | Basic salary |



\### Fringe Benefits Tax (FBT)



\- Register if providing non-cash benefits to employees

\- Benefits include: housing, vehicles, loans, insurance

\## 📁 Record Keeping Requirements



\### What to Keep



| Record Type | Retention Period |

|-------------|------------------|

| \*\*Tax Invoices\*\* | 7 years |

| \*\*Purchase Invoices\*\* | 7 years |

| \*\*Sales Records\*\* | 7 years |

| \*\*Bank Statements\*\* | 7 years |

| \*\*Employee Records\*\* | 7 years |

| \*\*PAYE Records\*\* | 7 years |

| \*\*VAT Returns\*\* | 7 years |

| \*\*Financial Statements\*\* | 7 years |



\### Tax Invoice Requirements



A valid tax invoice must include:

1\. "Tax Invoice" clearly stated

2\. Supplier's name, address, and TIN

3\. Customer's name, address, and TIN

4\. Invoice date and number

5\. Description of goods/services

6\. Quantity and price

7\. VAT amount

8\. Total amount including VAT

\## ⚠️ Penalties \& How to Avoid Them



\### Common Penalties



| Violation | Penalty |

|-----------|---------|

| \*\*Late TIN registration\*\* | Fine up to $1,000 |

| \*\*Late VAT registration\*\* | Fine up to $5,000 + interest |

| \*\*Late VAT filing\*\* | 10% of tax due + interest |

| \*\*Late PAYE remittance\*\* | 10% of amount due |

| \*\*Late FNPF contributions\*\* | 10% + interest |

| \*\*Incorrect VAT return\*\* | 50% of underpaid tax |

| \*\*Failing to keep records\*\* | Fine up to $5,000 |



\### How to Avoid Penalties



1\. \*\*Set reminders for all deadlines\*\*

2\. \*\*Keep accurate records from day one\*\*

3\. \*\*File returns on time, even if you can't pay\*\*

4\. \*\*Seek professional advice when unsure\*\*

5\. \*\*Register for TPOS Online Services for easy filing\*\*



\### Interest Rates



\- \*\*Standard Interest:\*\* 10% per annum

\- \*\*Penalty Interest:\*\* Additional 10% for late payment

\- \*\*Compounding:\*\* Interest compounds daily

\## ✅ Checklist Summary



\### Before You Start



\- \[ ] Register business with ROC

\- \[ ] Obtain Certificate of Registration

\- \[ ] Register for TIN on TPOS

\- \[ ] Set up business bank account



\### When You Reach $50,000 Turnover



\- \[ ] Register for VAT Monitoring System (VMS)

\- \[ ] Monitor turnover regularly



\### When You Reach $100,000 Turnover



\- \[ ] Register for VAT within 21 days

\- \[ ] Start charging VAT on invoices

\- \[ ] Begin filing VAT returns monthly or quarterly



\### When You Hire Employees



\- \[ ] Register as employer for PAYE within 30 days

\- \[ ] Register with FNPF within 30 days

\- \[ ] Register for FBT if providing benefits



\### Monthly Tasks



\- \[ ] PAYE remittance (by 14th)

\- \[ ] FNPF contributions (by last day)

\- \[ ] VAT return (if Category A)



\### Quarterly Tasks



\- \[ ] VAT return (if Category B)



\### Annual Tasks



\- \[ ] Corporate tax return (by 31 March)

\- \[ ] Financial statements

\- \[ ] Employee tax certificates (by 31 May)
